Code of Conduct

Do's

  • Be prepared and organized
  • Provide regular feedback
  • Communicate clearly and promptly
  • Respect cultural differences
  • Continuously improve your teaching skills

Don'ts

  • Miss scheduled sessions without notice
  • Share personal contact information
  • Use inappropriate language or content
  • Discriminate against any student
  • Accept payments outside the platform

Guideline Violations

Failure to follow these guidelines may result in:

Warning and required training
Temporary suspension from the platform
Permanent removal from TutorHub
