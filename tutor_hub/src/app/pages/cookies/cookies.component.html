Cookie Policy

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by:

  • Remembering your preferences
  • Keeping you logged in
  • Understanding how you use our site
  • Providing personalized content

Types of Cookies We Use

We use several types of cookies on TutorHub:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They include:

  • Authentication cookies
  • Security cookies
  • Session management cookies

Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

  • Google Analytics cookies
  • Page load time tracking
  • Error reporting cookies

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as videos and live chats.

  • Language preference cookies
  • Theme preference cookies
  • Remembering your login information

Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used to track visitors across websites to display ads that are relevant and engaging for the individual user.

  • Advertising tracking cookies
  • Social media integration cookies
  • Retargeting cookies

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To personalize user experience
  • To analyze website traffic and usage
  • To improve our services
  • To provide relevant advertisements
  • To ensure security and prevent fraud

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish. You can:

  • Delete all cookies that are already on your computer
  • Set most browsers to prevent cookies from being placed
  • Use our cookie consent banner to manage preferences
  • Opt-out of third-party cookies through their respective platforms

Note: Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website and limit your access to certain features.

Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services that may place their own cookies on your device. These include:

  • Google Analytics: For website analytics
  • Facebook Pixel: For advertising and retargeting
  • Google AdSense: For displaying relevant ads
  • Payment Gateways: For secure payment processing

Cookie Duration

Different cookies have different lifespans:

  • Session Cookies: Expire when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them
  • Authentication Cookies: Typically last 30 days
  • Analytics Cookies: Typically last 2 years

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what cookies are being used
  • Accept or reject non-essential cookies
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request information about cookies stored on your device
